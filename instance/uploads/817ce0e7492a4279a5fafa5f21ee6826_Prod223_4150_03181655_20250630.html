Strategic Report for the Year Ended 30 June 2025

The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 30 June 2025.

Principal activity

The principal activity of the Group is the provision of wholesale trade of motor vehicle parts and accessories.

Fair review of the business

The group experienced an upturn in trade on the previous year and despite a significant increase in staff costs, margins remain consistent with a slight increase in percentage. The focus this year has been on consolidation and strengthening the operational structure within the group, with a particular emphasis on processes, with the objective of achieving ISO accreditation in 2026. CSD Friction Limited has continued to perform steadily and Farbex Refinish UK Limited has seen significant year on year growth, we expect this to continue in the financial year to 2026.

Principal risks and uncertainties

The primary risk of the business is the loss of the Group's good reputation within the industry. This risk is managed by working closely with customers, continuously enhancing services and improving service levels, and by gaining business across a wider customer base.

Section 172(1) statement

The directors aim to act in a way that promotes the long-term success of the business for the benefit of all its stakeholders. Section 172 matters are considered in our strategy development and significant decisions across all aspects of our business.

Section 172 requires directors to have regard to:
• The likely consequences of any decision in the long term
• The interests of the company's employees
• The need to foster the company's business relationships with suppliers, customers and others
• The impact of the company's operations on the community and the environment
• The desirability of the company maintaining a reputation for high standards of business conduct, and
• The need to act fairly between members of the company.

The directors are aware of their responsibilities to ensure that the company acts fairly in all its dealings and communications with key stakeholder groups and considers the impact of the company’s operations on the wider community and the environment. The directors consider a range of factors in making decisions on the success of the company and its holdings in the group, including the section 172 duties above, the company’s activities and, where appropriate, the subsidiaries' business.

Engagement with suppliers, customers and other relationships

We communicate with customers and suppliers to create long-term value for our stakeholders by providing industry-leading expertise and solutions and through the efficient use of resources.

Payments to creditors

It is the company's policy to settle the terms of payment with suppliers when agreeing the terms of each transaction. It is the company's policy to ensure that suppliers are made aware of the terms of payment, and that we abide by those terms. There are different terms agreed within the company's supplier base, averaging 60 days taken to pay trade creditors.

Price risk, credit risk, liquidity risk and cash flow risk

Price
The group has minimal exposure to price risk as all prices are pre-set by management.

Credit
The group is exposed to credit risk and management ensure that credit checks are completed on all new customers and chase debts on a regular basis once they become overdue.

Cash flow
The group is exposed to cash flow risk as a result of the timing between paying suppliers and the receipt of money from customers. Management manage this through the use of an invoice discounting facility.
